FEATURED · ANDROID

Procedural Ball Platformer

A landscape side-scroller built around a physics-driven ball, touch input, trajectory prediction, and a terrain path that extends as the player advances.

Unity PhysicsTouch InputProcedural TerrainImpact Deformation

Implementation highlights

  • Spawns and removes terrain segments as the camera advances, keeping active terrain bounded.
  • Uses a controlled range of ground-height variation and a predefined visual sequence for time-of-day transitions.
  • Includes jump trajectory prediction, collision-responsive squash-and-stretch, score progression, and a game-over/restart loop.

Technical scope: the ball uses Unity physics for movement and collisions; squash-and-stretch is an impact-responsive visual effect, not a custom soft-body solver.
